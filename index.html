The protocol patchbay

Like a studio engineer's patchbay, fluxrig normalizes your ecosystem—legacy or modern, edge or cloud—into a harmonized, real-time control plane. We decouple your business logic from the "black box" of industrial and financial protocols, allowing you to innovate without the friction of proprietary systems.

Declarative Pipeline Management

Deploy production-ready ISO8583 interfaces and routing rules in minutes via YAML configuration. fluxrig is a declarative platform, not a code-heavy framework.

Single-Binary Excellence

Lightweight Go binaries (<50MB) with zero external dependencies. Powered by NATS JetStream for resilient, stateful communication in disconnected environments.

Unified observability

Bring modern tracing (OpenTelemetry) to 24/7 mission-critical flows. Link every signal to a unified trace ID across your entire distributed fleet.

Built for the physical world

Whether you are managing payment gateways, industrial sensors, or healthcare equipment, fluxrig provides the precise control plane needed for orchestration at scale.
